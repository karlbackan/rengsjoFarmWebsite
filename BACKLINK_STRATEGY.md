# Backlink Strategy for Rengsjö Farm - 2024-2025

## Overview
This document outlines a comprehensive backlink strategy tailored specifically for Rengsjö Farm, a family-owned lamb farm in Hälsingland, Sweden. The strategy focuses on building high-quality, relevant backlinks that will improve local SEO rankings and drive targeted traffic to the website.

## What Are Backlinks and Why They Matter

**Backlinks** = Other websites linking to your website (rengsjöfarm.se)

### Impact on SEO:
- **Top 3 ranking factor** alongside content quality and user experience
- Can mean the difference between page 10 and page 1 in search results
- Especially crucial for local searches like "lammkött hälsingland"
- Acts as "digital word-of-mouth" recommendations

### Quality Over Quantity:
- 1 link from Bollnäs newspaper > 100 links from random sites
- Google's 2024 updates prioritize link quality over quantity
- Links from trusted local sources carry more weight

## Priority Backlink Opportunities for Rengsjö Farm

### Tier 1: High-Priority Local Links (Start Immediately)

#### 1. **Municipal and Tourism Sites**
- **Bollnäs Kommun** - Tourism/local business directory
- **Visit Hälsingland** - Regional tourism website
- **Destination Bollnäs** - Local tourism promotion
- **Action:** Contact tourism offices, emphasize farm visits and local food production

#### 2. **Local News Media**
- **Ljusnan** (local newspaper)
- **Bollnäs Tidning**
- **Hälsinge-Kuriren**
- **Story angles:**
  - 30+ years of family farming tradition
  - Sustainable lamb production practices
  - Seasonal stories (lambing season, harvest)
  - Local food movement participation

#### 3. **Agricultural Organizations**
- **LRF Gävleborg** (Lantbrukarnas Riksförbund)
- **Hushållningssällskapet Gävleborg**
- **Svenska Fåravelsförbundet**
- **KRAV** (if organic certified)
- **Action:** Get listed in member directories, contribute articles

#### 4. **Local Business Networks**
- **Företagarna Bollnäs**
- **Bollnäs Näringslivskontor**
- **Handelskammaren Mittsverige**
- **Action:** Join, participate in events, get featured in member spotlights

### Tier 2: Food and Culinary Links

#### 5. **Restaurant Partnerships**
- Restaurants using your lamb
- Farm-to-table establishments in Gävleborg
- **Strategy:** Offer to be featured as supplier on their websites
- **Example text:** "Vi använder lokalt lammkött från Rengsjö Farm"

#### 6. **Food Bloggers and Influencers**
- Swedish food bloggers focusing on local/sustainable food
- Recipe creators using lamb
- **Approach:** Offer product samples for honest reviews
- **Target:** Bloggers within 200km radius first

#### 7. **Culinary Organizations**
- **Matlandethälsingland.se**
- **Smaka på Gävleborg**
- **Bondens egen Marknad**
- **Lokalproducerat i Sverige**

### Tier 3: Community and Social Impact

#### 8. **Local Events and Sponsorships**
- Sponsor local sports teams
- Participate in harvest festivals
- Support school agriculture programs
- **Benefit:** Event websites link to sponsors

#### 9. **Sustainability and Environmental Sites**
- **Naturskyddsföreningen** (local chapter)
- Sustainable farming directories
- Climate-friendly food initiatives
- **Angle:** Natural grazing, low-impact farming

#### 10. **Educational Partnerships**
- Agricultural schools in region
- University research on sustainable farming
- School visit programs
- **Opportunity:** Case studies, research citations

## Swedish SEO Compliance Note

**VIKTIGT:** Enligt Google's riktlinjer får du inte:
- Betala för länkar
- Byta länkar ("du länkar till mig, jag länkar till dig")
- Använda automatiserade länkbyggande verktyg
- Skapa onaturliga länkmönster

Fokusera alltid på att bygga naturliga, värdefulla relationer inom lokalsamhället.
