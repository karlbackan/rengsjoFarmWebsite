# SEO Next Steps for Rengsjö Farm

## Priority 1: Google Business Profile (Immediate Impact)

### Why It's Critical
- Gets you on Google Maps and in local search results within days
- Shows your business directly in search results with map, hours, and contact info
- Enables "near me" searches (e.g., "lammkött near me", "farm near me")
- Free and most impactful for local businesses

### How to Set Up
1. Go to [business.google.com](https://business.google.com)
2. Sign in with a Google account
3. Enter business name: **Rengsjö Farm**
4. Address: **Östra Höle 9326, 821 98 Rengsjö**
5. Category: Choose "Farm" or "Meat wholesaler"
6. Add phone: **073-801 93 51**
7. Add website: **https://rengsjofarm.se**
8. Verify your business (Google will send a postcard with verification code)

### What to Add After Verification
- **Photos**: Upload 10-20 high-quality photos of:
  - Your sheep/lambs grazing
  - The farm buildings
  - Meat products
  - Nina and Thomas (owners)
- **Business hours**: Mo-Su 08:00-20:00
- **Business description**: Copy from website
- **Products**: Add your lamb products with descriptions
- **Attributes**: "Family-owned", "Parking available"

2. Add property: **https://rengsjofarm.se**

## Priority 6: Review Management

   > "Tack för din beställning! Om du är nöjd skulle vi uppskatta om du lämnade en recension på Google."

2. **Make it easy**: Send direct link to your Google Business Profile review page

3. **Respond to all reviews**: Thank positive reviews, address any concerns professionally

### Where Reviews Matter Most
- Google Business Profile (most important)
- Facebook page (if you have one)
- Your website (can add testimonials section)
